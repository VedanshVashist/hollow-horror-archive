# Code of Conduct

The Hollow is a small, welcoming space for people who enjoy making things together. We want participation to be safe and respectful, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that help the community include:

- Being considerate and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Welcoming beginners and making room for different perspectives.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, threats, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Handling a problem

If a conversation or contribution violates these expectations, contact a project maintainer privately through the contact options on the repository host. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may block participation when needed to keep the space respectful.

This code applies to project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

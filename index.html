A small archive

The Hollow is a collection of original, bite-sized ghost stories and strange little scenes. Every illustration is drawn in SVG right here in the project—no image APIs, no tracking, no lost signal required.

The archive is open source. Bring a new haunting, a better way to read in the dark, or just a curious mind. The door sticks, but it opens.
